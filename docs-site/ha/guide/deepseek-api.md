# Samu maɓallin DeepSeek API

Deepsand yana buƙatar mai bada samfuri don kunna mataimaki. Tsarin tsoho yana amfani da DeepSeek. Wannan shafin yana tafiya ta hanyar ƙirƙirar asusu, ƙara kuɗi, yin maɓallin API, da adana shi a cikin Deepsand.

Idan kana da maɓallin API daga wani mai badawa, tsallake zuwa [Yi amfani da maɓallin da kuke da shi](#use-a-key-you-already-have).

## Kafin ka fara

Kuna buƙatar:

- Adireshin imel ko asusun shiga mai goyan bayan.
- Hanyar biyan kuɗi don kiredit API.
- Mintuna kaɗan don ƙirƙirar maɓallin kuma manna shi cikin Deepsand.

Ana cajin DeepSeek API daban daga gidan yanar gizon taɗi na DeepSeek da aikace-aikacen hannu. Ƙara ƙididdiga akan dandalin API shine abin da ke bawa mataimaki damar yin buƙatu.

## 1. Ƙirƙiri asusun DeepSeek

1. Bude [DeepSeek API dandamali](https://platform.deepseek.com/).
2. Zaɓi **Yi rajista** ko **Shiga**.
3. Ƙirƙiri asusu tare da adireshin imel ɗin ku, ko amfani da ɗaya daga cikin zaɓuɓɓukan shiga masu goyan bayan.
4. Tabbatar da adireshin imel ɗinku ko lambar wayarku idan dandalin ya neme ku.

## 2. Ƙara bashi

1. Shiga zuwa dandalin API.
2. Bude lissafin lissafin kuɗi ko ɓangaren sama a cikin dashboard.
3. Ƙara adadin kuɗin da kuke son amfani da su.
4. Kammala biyan kuɗi.

An cire amfani da API daga wannan ma'auni. Dandalin yana nuna ma'auni na yanzu, amfani da farashi. Fara da ƙaramin adadin yayin da kuke ƙoƙarin Deepsand; za ku iya ƙarawa daga baya.

## 3. Ƙirƙiri maɓallin API

1. Bude sashen **Maɓallan API** na dandalin.
2. Zaɓi **Ƙirƙiri sabon maɓallin API** ko maɓallin daidai.
3. Ka ba maɓalli suna da za ka gane, kamar **Saukewa: Deepsand**.
4. Ƙirƙiri maɓallin kuma kwafi shi nan da nan.

Ana nuna maɓalli sau ɗaya kawai. Yi la'akari da shi kamar kalmar sirri: duk wanda ke da shi zai iya kashe kuɗin kuɗi a asusunku.

## 4. Ajiye maɓalli a cikin Deepsand

1. Bude Deepsand.
2. Bude **Saituna**.
3. Nemo filin **Maɓallin API** a ƙarƙashin tsoffin takaddun shaida.
4. Manna maɓallin.
5. Zaɓi **Ajiye maɓalli**.

Deepsand yana adana maɓalli a maɓallan tsarin aikin ku, ba a cikin fayil ɗin saitin sa ba. Tagar tana nuna haruffa huɗu na ƙarshe bayan adanawa, saboda haka zaku iya tabbatar da maɓalli yana nan ba tare da sake fallasa shi ba.

Ana amfani da maɓalli mai faɗin ƙa'idar lokacin da mai masaukin DeepCode ba zai samar da nasa maɓalli ba. Maɓallin matakin aikin har yanzu yana kan gaba.

## Yi amfani da maɓallin da kuke da shi

Idan kun riga kuna da maɓallin API, ba kwa buƙatar ƙirƙirar asusun DeepSeek.

- Don amfani da shi don kowane aiki, buɗe **Saituna**, shigar da maɓallin a cikin filin **Maɓallin API**, kuma adana shi.
- Don amfani da wani wuri na ƙarshe ko samfuri daban, kuma saita tushen URL da samfuri a cikin **Saituna** ko cikin takaddun shaidar aikin.
- Don ajiye maɓalli don aiki ɗaya kawai, buɗe saitunan aikin kuma shigar da shi a maimakon haka.

Dole ne mai badawa ya fallasa API wanda DeepCode CLI zai iya kira. Idan kana amfani da mai ba da DeepSeek, shigar da tushe URL da sunan ƙira maimakon madaidaicin DeepSeek.

## Idan wani abu bai yi aiki ba

- **An ƙi maɓalli:** ƙirƙiri sabon maɓalli akan dandamali kuma maye gurbin wanda aka adana.
- **Ma'auni fanko ne:** ƙara daraja zuwa asusun API. Biyan kuɗin taɗi baya ba da kuɗin amfani da API ta atomatik.
- **Mataimakin ba zai iya isa ga mai bayarwa ba:** duba yanayin hanyar sadarwa na aikin da jerin izini. Dole ne a iya kaiwa ga ƙarshen samfurin.
- **Kuna son cire maɓallin:** ya buɗe **Saituna** kuma zaɓi **Cire maɓalli**.
