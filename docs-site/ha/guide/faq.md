# Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

## Shin DeepCode yana ganin kundin adireshi na gida?

A'a. Akwatin yashi yana ganin kundin tsarin aiki a `/workspace` da kundayen adireshi da kuka bayar a sarari. Sauran tsarin fayil ɗin rundunar ba a hawa ba.

## Me zai faru idan na amince da buƙatun shugabanci?

Deepsand yana ƙara kundin adireshi azaman kyauta, yana sake ƙirƙirar akwati tare da sabon dutsen da aka makala, kuma ya ci gaba da zama na DeepCode. Akwatin yashi mai gudana ba zai iya samun dutsen ɗaure ba tare da an sake ƙirƙira shi ba.

## A ina ake ajiye hirara?

Tarihin tattaunawa, zama, haɗe-haɗe da ƙwarewar da aka ƙirƙira suna rayuwa a cikin kowane aikin Docker. Babban fayil ɗin aikin akan faifai ya ƙunshi lambar ku da fayilolinku kawai.

Cire aiki daga Deepsand baya share wannan ƙarar. Shafa bayanan DeepCode na aikin yana cire ƙarar amma yana barin babban fayil ɗin aikin ba a taɓa shi ba.

## Ina ake adana maɓallai da sirrikan API?

Ana rufaffen sirri yayin hutawa tare da maɓalli da ke riƙe da maɓalli na tsarin aiki, kuma ana allura a cikin akwatin yashi azaman masu canjin yanayi kawai idan ya fara. Keɓancewar yana nuna sunaye da tsayi, ba koyaushe ana adana ƙimar ba.

Hakanan za'a iya gadon takaddun shaida daga tsarin DeepCode mai masaukin ku. Ba a kwafi su cikin babban fayil ɗin aikin.

## Shin kowane aikin zai iya amfani da samfurin daban?

Ee. Aiki na iya gaji daidaitawar mai masaukin baki ko ƙetare samfurin, URL tushe da takaddun shaida a cikin saitunan sa. Hakanan mai zaɓin taken yana iya zaɓar samfuri don aikin; **Tsara zuwa Config** yana nufin aikin baya liƙa ɗaya.

## Wane samfurin zan yi amfani da shi don rage farashi?

Yi amfani da ƙirar walƙiya don yawancin ayyuka. Deepsand ya sabawa `deepseek-v4-flash`, wanda shine mafi arha zaɓi don yin rikodin yau da kullun, bayani da gyarawa. Ajiye `deepseek-v4-pro` don aiki inda walƙiya ke fafitikar fafitika ko kuna buƙatar ƙarin ƙarfinsa, saboda ƙirar ƙirar ƙira don aiki.

Canja samfuri daga kan aikin ko tare da `/model` a cikin tashar DeepCode. Idan kuna ƙoƙarin Deepsand a karon farko, fara da walƙiya kuma kawai matsa zuwa pro lokacin da kuke da aikin da ke buƙatar sa.

## Menene ma'aunin tunani yake yi?

Madogarar tunani tana kusa da mai zaɓin ƙira a cikin taken aikin. Yana sarrafa yawan dalilan da ƙirar ke yi kafin ya ba da amsa.

| Tsaya | Abin da ake nufi |
| --- | --- |
| **Kashe** | Kar ka yi tunani kafin ka ba da amsa. Mafi sauri, amma mafi ƙarancin hankali. |
| **Mai sauri** | Tunani kaɗan, don ƙananan ƙayyadaddun gyare-gyare. |
| **Daidaitacce** | Matsakaicin tunani. Tsayawa ta tsakiya da madaidaicin ma'ana don aikin gaske. |
| **Zurfafa** | Yi tunani sosai kamar yadda samfurin zai iya. A hankali kuma mafi tsada. |
| **Tsohuwar** | Tsaya zuwa ga mai watsa shiri `settings.json`; idan bai ce komai ba, DeepCode ya koma Balanced. |

Ajiye faifai akan **Daidaitacce** don yawancin ayyuka. Yana da saitin tsakiya, kuma yana ba da sakamako mai kyau ba tare da biyan kuɗi mafi hankali ba, mafi tsada dalili akan kowace bukata. Matsar zuwa **Zurfafa** kawai don matsaloli masu wuyar gaske, kuma yi amfani da **Kashe** ko **Mai sauri** don ƙarami, gyare-gyaren tsinkaya.

## Shin Deepsand yana aiki tare da DeepSeek kawai?

A'a. DeepSeek shine tsoho saboda shine samfurin da ƙarshen Deepsand jiragen ruwa tare da, amma Deepsand yana daidaita DeepCode CLI tare da sunan samfurin, URL tushe da maɓallin API. Kuna iya nuna shi zuwa wani mai badawa wanda ke fallasa API wanda CLI zai iya kira, yawanci madaidaicin buɗaɗɗen ƙarshen ƙarshen.

Saita tushen URL na mai badawa da samfurin a cikin **Saituna**, ko a cikin takaddun shaidar aikin, kuma adana maɓallin API na mai badawa a wuri guda. Dole ne manufar hanyar sadarwa ta sandbox kuma ta ba da damar ƙarshen mai samarwa.

## Idan Docker Desktop baya gudana ko ya daina amsawa fa?

Deepsand ba zai iya fara akwatin yashi ba tare da injin Docker ba. Idan Docker Desktop baya aiki, kunna shi kuma jira injin ya gama farawa. Deepsand yana sake duba lokacin da taga ta dawo da hankali, kuma tsiri matsayi yana ba da sake gwadawa.

Wani lokaci Docker Desktop yana aiki amma injinsa ya daina amsawa. Sake kunna Deepsand baya gyara cewa; Docker Desktop kanta yana buƙatar sake kunnawa.

- **Yi amfani da sarrafa in-app lokacin da ya bayyana.** Idan Deepsand ya gano Docker Desktop, yana nuna **Sake kunna Docker Desktop…** a cikin matsayi ko faɗakarwa. Wannan ikon yana sake kunna injin Docker, gami da tsarin WSL da yake amfani da shi akan Windows, kuma yana jira har sai akwati ya sake farawa.
- **Windows:** dama-danna Docker whale a cikin sanarwar kuma zaɓi **Sake kunnawa**. Idan wannan zaɓi ya ɓace, buɗe Docker Desktop, zaɓi **Shirya matsala**, sannan **Sake kunna Docker Desktop**.
- **macOS:** danna Docker whale a cikin mashaya menu kuma zaɓi **Sake kunnawa**.
- **Linux:** buɗe menu na Docker Desktop, zaɓi **Shirya matsala**, sannan **Sake kunna Docker Desktop**. Idan kuna amfani da Docker Engine maimakon Docker Desktop, sake kunna sabis ɗin, misali `sudo systemctl restart docker`.

Sake kunna Docker Desktop yana ƙare kowane akwati akan injin, gami da kwantena waɗanda basu da alaƙa da Deepsand. Deepsand yana ba da sake farawa amma baya yin ta ta atomatik. Duba [Takaddun matsala na Docker](https://docs.docker.com/desktop/troubleshoot-and-support/troubleshoot/) don takamaiman menu na dandamali.

## Zan iya amfani da Deepsand ba tare da shiga intanet ba?

Samfurin yana buƙatar ƙarshen ƙarshensa. Tare da yanayin hanyar sadarwa na `none`, ƙarshen ƙarshen kawai ana iya kaiwa ta hanyar wakili na Deepsand. Shigar da fakitin da sauran damar hanyar sadarwa ba za su gaza ba sai dai idan kun ƙyale rundunonin da suke buƙata.

## Ta yaya zan sabunta Deepsand?

Bude **Saituna**, sannan **Sabuntawa**, kuma duba sabon sigar. Sakin yana gina sabbin abubuwan da aka sanya hannu a zazzage kuma a sake kunna app bayan shigar da ɗaya. Shigar da sabuntawa yana dakatar da gudanar da akwatunan yashi.

## Akwai layin umarni?

Ee. `deepsandctl` yana ba da umarni marasa kai kamar `doctor`, `plan`, `run`, `watch`, `requests` da `network`. Aikace-aikacen tebur da CLI suna raba lambar manufofin iri ɗaya a cikin `deepsand-core`.

## Ta yaya zan bayar da rahoton batun tsaro?

Kar a bude batun jama'a don matsalar tsaro. Tuntuɓi masu kula a keɓance ta tashar bayar da rahoton tsaro na ma'ajiyar kuma haɗa da cikakkun bayanai don sake haifar da matsalar.
