# Yadda akwatin yashi ke aiki

A bayyane: mataimaki na iya aiki a cikin aikin da kuka zaɓa da kuma a cikin manyan fayilolin da kuka yarda, kuma babu wani wuri a kan kwamfutarka.

Alkawarin Deepsand kunkuntar ne kuma abin iya gwadawa: akwatin yashi na iya isa ga kundin tsarin aiki da kundayen adireshi da kuka bayar a sarari, kuma ba komai ba.

## Iyakar

Kwanin ba shi da ra'ayi na tsarin fayil ɗin mai watsa shiri ban da ɗaurin ɗaure Deepsand ya zaɓa. Sauran hanyoyin masaukin ba su wanzu a cikin wurin tudun sunaye na kwantena, don haka babu fayil ɗin masaukin da zai tsere zuwa.

Duk abin da aka kwatanta anan shine tsaro a cikin zurfin kan iyakar.

## Abin da aka tilasta

- Wurin aiki kawai da bayyanun tallafi ana hawa daga mai masaukin baki.
- An ɗora spool mai amsawa da kundin adireshi na jihohi karatu-kawai.
- An ƙi soketsan lokacin kwantena da suna.
- Tushen tsarin fayil ɗin ganga ana karantawa kawai, tare da girman tsarin fayil na wucin gadi don `/tmp` da `/run`.
- Ana warware hanyoyin haɗin gwiwa kafin a bincika hanya, don haka hanyar haɗin yanar gizo ba za ta iya ɓad da abin da aka haramta ba.
- Ana watsi da gata kafin DeepCode ya gudana, kuma `no-new-privileges` yana hana a dawo dasu.

## Hanyoyin sadarwa

| Yanayin | Abin da ya halatta |
| --- | --- |
| Babu | Ƙarshen ƙirar ƙirar kawai ta hanyar wakili na Deepsand. |
| An halatta | Ƙarshen ƙirar ƙirar, wakili, da runduna ko adireshi akan jerin izini. |
| Intanet | Samun damar Intanet, tare da toshe kewayon gida da na sirri a cikin akwati. |
| Cikakkun | Samun damar Intanet ba tare da toshe cibiyar sadarwar gida ba. Yi amfani da shi kawai lokacin da akwatin yashi yana buƙatar ainihin hanyar sadarwar ku ta gida. |

Sunan mai ba da izini yana wucewa ta hanyar wakili, wanda ke warware sunan da kansa kuma ya ƙi sakamako a sararin adireshi na sirri. Ana ba da izinin adireshin da aka ba da izini kai tsaye ta dokokin kwantena, saboda abokin ciniki na bayanai ko wasu shirye-shiryen da ba na HTTP ba zai iya amfani da wakili. Hakanan ana buga tashar jiragen ruwa mai suna a cikin shigarwa akan madaidaicin madaidaicin mai watsa shiri, ba a kan kowane ma'amala ba.

## Abin da kuka yanke shawara

Deepsand baya bada dama ta atomatik. Buƙatar ta isa gare ku da hanya, mai masaukin baki, kayan aiki ko albarkatun da ake nema, kuma akwatin yashi yana tsayawa har sai kun yanke shawara.

An ƙi buƙatar wani abu da akwatin yashi ya rigaya ya samu tare da bayani, saboda izini ta atomatik zai zama izinin da ba ku taɓa bayarwa ba. Duk wani abu mai ban sha'awa ya isa gare ku.

## Abin da ba ya karewa

- Mai bada samfurin zai iya ganin abun ciki da ka aika masa.
- Fayilolin da kuka sanya a cikin filin aiki suna bayyane ga akwatin yashi kuma ana iya canza su ko share su.
- Kayan aiki mara kyau ko koyarwar ƙira na iya neman shiga. Hukuncin ya kasance naku.
- Docker kanta tana wajen iyaka. Ci gaba da sabunta Docker Desktop da tsarin aiki mai watsa shiri.

## Kara karantawa

Cikakken samfurin tsaro, gami da gwaje-gwajen da ke tabbatar da kowane kadara, yana cikin [docs/TSARO.md](https://github.com/L1lith/Deepsand/blob/master/docs/SECURITY.md).

An kwatanta gine-ginen a cikin [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](https://github.com/L1lith/Deepsand/blob/master/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
